Research Project

Ongoing — 2025

AI-Based Driver Behavior Analysis
& Accident Risk Prediction

This research focuses on predicting accident risk in Sri Lanka using artificial intelligence. It analyses driver behaviour — including drowsiness, distraction, stress, and attention level — through machine learning models trained on GPS data, weather conditions, and historical accident records. The system classifies risk levels (low, medium, high) and provides real-time alerts with safe alternative routes via Google Maps API integration.
